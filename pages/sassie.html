Intro

The Semi-submersible Array and Sampler for Sewage Impact on Ecosystems (SASSIE) drone was a third year project in university. It was developed by the SASDEV team consisting of myself and another fellow robotics student with the aide of 3 computing students. The aim of the project was to develop a system which could be deployed into shallow waters and collect samples to analyse the impact sewage is having on marine life. This would also mean that volunteers wouldn't have to manually grab samples from the ocean. Only a prototype was built with the full version having many more planned features such as the ability to fully submerge. However the prototype was an appropriate MVP as it could do the full sampling cycle.
