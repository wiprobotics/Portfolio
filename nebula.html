Nebula Arduino Hat

A servo breakout board for Arduino and compatible microcontrollers

About the Project

As a part of the HI5 project, we needed a way to control multiple servos with precise timing. The Nebula Arduino Hat was designed to provide an easy-to-use solution for controlling up to 12 servos from an Arduino with dedicated power regulation and signal integrity.

The board started as a 5 servo breakout just for the HI5 hand, but was later expanded to 12 servos to accommodate more complex projects. It features a dedicated 5V power supply input, ensuring that the servos receive stable power without overloading the Arduino's onboard regulator.

The Nebula Hat is compatible with standard hobby servos and can be easily integrated into any Arduino project. It fits perfectly on top of an Arduino Uno or Mega, making it a compact and efficient solution for robotics and automation projects.
